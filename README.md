## 📌 Project Overview

FinTrust Digital Bank is a fictional digital banking organization looking to use data,
machine learning and generative AI to better understand customer and transaction
behaviour, support risk related decision-making, improve operational processes and
strengthen digital customer support.

This repository documents my work as a Data Analytics intern on the **FinTrust
Financial Intelligence & Digital Banking Support Solution**, a 4-week project that
progresses from business understanding through to a final interactive Power BI
dashboard.
